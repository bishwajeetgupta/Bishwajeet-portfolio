Overview

A digital marketing agency needed a complete website redesign that could clearly explain its digital marketing services and convert visitors into consultation bookings — the previous site buried its offerings under generic layout patterns.

The rebuild focused on structuring the site around what a visitor actually wants to know first — what services are offered, and how to get in touch — rather than around the agency's internal org chart.

  • Marketing / lead-generation website
  • WordPress (Elementor) implementation
  • SEO-friendly structure
  • Mobile-responsive, performance-focused

Design and build

I designed the full website UI in Figma, screen by screen — home, services, about, and contact. Bold headline type paired with restrained body copy kept the service pages easy to scan, with a call-to-action placed at every natural decision point.

Each screen was drawn for desktop first, then adjusted for tablet and mobile so the content order, spacing, and tap targets still worked on small screens. After the design was approved, I converted it into WordPress myself, page by page, keeping the same spacing, type sizes, and breakpoints as the Figma files.

  • Website UI design in Figma, all key pages
  • Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • Figma to WordPress conversion, built and shipped by me
  • Editable page sections so the client can update content without a developer

Challenges & solutions

Challenge: The old site buried the services under generic layout patterns, so visitors could not quickly see what the agency offered or how to book a consultation. Interested people were dropping off before they reached the contact step.

Solution: I restructured the site around what a visitor wants to know first, put the services and the booking path up front, and cut the number of steps to get in touch. The result was a much shorter path from interest to a booked consultation.

Conclusion

Every layout decision was made in service of one goal: get a visitor from "what does this agency do" to "book a consultation" in as few steps as possible.

Built and shipped end-to-end in WordPress, the site is fully responsive and structured for SEO — so the traffic that does land on the site has the best chance of converting.
