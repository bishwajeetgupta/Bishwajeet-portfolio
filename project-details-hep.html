Overview

HEP is CDL's central backend platform for managing projects, residential units, homebuyer information, payments, and appointments. I redesigned the end-to-end UI/UX of the platform, working closely with the development team to turn complex, multi-step admin workflows into interfaces that are clear and easy to operate day to day.

The platform serves multiple internal teams with different roles and permissions, so consistency across forms, tables, dashboards, and navigation was essential. I delivered responsive, developer-friendly designs and interactive prototypes in Figma throughout the project.

  • Project & residential unit management
  • Homebuyer information & payments
  • Appointment scheduling
  • Role-based admin workflows

Design system

I built a reusable design system with consistent components for forms, tables, dashboards, and navigation. Standardizing these pieces cut down design and build time on new screens and kept the admin experience consistent as the platform grew.

  • Shared components for forms, tables and navigation
  • Consistent colours, type and spacing
  • Faster design and build on new screens

Challenges & solutions

Challenge: The platform served several internal teams with different roles and permissions, and its screens had grown inconsistent over time. Staff had to relearn patterns as they moved between projects, units, payments, and appointments, which slowed daily work.

Solution: I audited the flows and set shared patterns for forms, tables, dashboards, and navigation, so every module looked and behaved the same way. This lowered the learning curve for staff and made the platform faster and safer for the developers to extend.

Conclusion

The redesigned HEP portal gives CDL's teams a consistent, easier-to-navigate way to manage projects, units, and homebuyers, backed by a design system that makes it faster to extend the platform going forward.

Close collaboration with the development team throughout the project meant designs translated directly into working screens, with less rework between design handoff and implementation.
