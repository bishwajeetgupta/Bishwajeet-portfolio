01 / Overview
OVERVIEW

This is an AI-driven hotel operations platform. It automates property tracking, inspections, maintenance workflows, task scheduling, cleaning operations, safety audits, asset monitoring and incident reporting. It is used across housekeeping, maintenance, supervisor and management roles.

The core challenge was to design one system that serves four very different daily workflows. Supervisors need oversight dashboards, while housekeeping and maintenance staff need a fast, mobile-first way to update task status from the field, often between rooms and with limited time.

Challenge The problem

One system had to serve four very different daily workflows. Supervisors needed oversight dashboards, while housekeeping and maintenance staff needed a fast, mobile-first way to update task status from the field, often between rooms and with very little time.

Solution The approach

I separated the views by role but built every screen from the same component library. Supervisors kept the oversight they needed, and field staff got a quick, mobile-first flow to update task status with fewer taps. As a result, no group felt that the tool was built for someone else.
