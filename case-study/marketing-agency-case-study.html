Overview

A digital marketing agency needed a complete website redesign. The new site had to clearly explain the agency's digital marketing services and turn visitors into consultation bookings. The previous site buried its services under generic layout patterns.

The rebuild structured the site around what a visitor wants to know first: what services are offered and how to get in touch. It was not built around the agency's internal team structure.

  • Marketing and lead-generation website
  • WordPress (Elementor) build
  • SEO-friendly structure
  • Mobile-responsive and performance-focused
Close-up: the Digital Marketing Services block on the Services page

Design and build

I designed the full website UI in Figma, screen by screen: home, services, about and contact. Bold headline type with calm body text kept the service pages easy to scan. A call to action sits at every natural decision point.

Each screen was designed for desktop first. Then I adjusted it for tablet and mobile, so the content order, spacing and tap targets still worked on small screens.

After the client approved the design, I built it in WordPress myself, page by page. I kept the same spacing, type sizes and breakpoints as the Figma files.

  • Website UI design in Figma for all key pages
  • Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Figma to WordPress conversion, built and shipped by me
  • Editable page sections, so the client can update content without a developer

Challenges & solutions

Challenge

The old site buried the services under generic layout patterns. Visitors could not quickly see what the agency offered or how to book a consultation. Interested people were dropping off before they reached the contact step.

Solution

I restructured the site around what a visitor wants to know first. I put the services and the booking path up front and cut the number of steps needed to get in touch. The result was a much shorter path from interest to a booked consultation.

Conclusion

Every layout decision served one goal: to get a visitor from "What does this agency do?" to "Book a consultation" in as few steps as possible.

I built and shipped the site end to end in WordPress. It is fully responsive and structured for SEO, so the traffic that lands on the site has the best chance of converting.
