Overview

This is a real estate developer's central backend platform for managing projects, residential units, homebuyer information, payments and appointments. I redesigned the full UI/UX of the platform. I worked closely with the development team to turn complex, multi-step admin workflows into clear screens that are easy to use every day.

The platform serves several internal teams with different roles and permissions. Because of this, consistency across forms, tables, dashboards and navigation was essential. Throughout the project, I delivered responsive, developer-friendly designs and interactive prototypes in Figma.

Challenges & solutions

Audit

Challenge

The platform served several internal teams with different roles and permissions. Over time, its screens had become inconsistent. Staff had to relearn patterns as they moved between projects, units, payments and appointments, and this slowed down daily work.

Standard

Solution

I audited the flows and set shared patterns for forms, tables, dashboards and navigation. As a result, every module looked and behaved the same way. This made the platform easier for staff to learn, and faster and safer for the developers to extend.

Conclusion

The redesigned portal gives the client's teams a consistent and easier way to manage projects, units and homebuyers. It is backed by a design system that makes the platform faster to extend in the future.

I worked closely with the development team throughout the project. Because of this, the designs turned directly into working screens, with less rework between design handoff and build.
