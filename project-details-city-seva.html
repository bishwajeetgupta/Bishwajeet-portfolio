Overview

An academic institution wanted an app for the residents of its city. With it, people could easily find the information and services they need in daily life, such as education, health and transportation. The institution offered the app as a public service to local citizens.

The client did not want a ready-made product. They wanted a simple, easy-to-use app with minimal navigation and a custom look and feel. I designed the user interface for the web portal and for the iOS and Android apps. I kept every screen clear, so residents could quickly find the service they need.

  • UI design for the web portal and mobile apps
  • Simple navigation and a clean custom interface
  • Service screens grouped around resident needs
  • Delivered in about 3 months

Design approach

The app gives city residents one place to reach important services. I kept the interface basic and easy to use, so people can find what they need with as few taps as possible. Requirements were discussed over email, Zoom calls and face-to-face meetings. I shared the screens for review as the app took shape.

  • Clear, flat navigation for a wide public audience
  • Plain labels and simple screen layouts
  • Consistent visual style across web and mobile
  • Service information grouped the way residents think

Challenges & solutions

Challenge

The app had to serve everyday residents of many ages, with different comfort levels using phones. The client asked for minimal navigation, so people needed to reach education, health and transport information without any confusion.

Solution

I kept the structure flat and the labels plain. I grouped the services the way residents actually think about them and reduced the taps needed to reach each service. This kept the app simple and easy to use for a wide public audience.

The design was delivered in about 3 months. The client shared a completion certificate at the end.
