Overview

An academic institution wanted an app for the residents of its city, so people could easily find the information and services they need in daily life such as education, health, and transportation. The institution offered this as a public service to the local citizens.

The client did not want to use any ready-made product. They wanted a simple, easy to use app with minimum navigation and a custom look and feel. I designed the user interface for the web portal and the iOS and Android apps, keeping every screen clear so residents could find the service they need quickly.

  • UI design for the web portal and mobile apps
  • Simple navigation and a clean custom interface
  • Service screens grouped around resident needs
  • Delivered in about 3 months

Design approach

The app gives city residents one place to reach important services. I focused on keeping the interface basic and easy to use, so people can find what they need with as few taps as possible. Requirements were discussed over email, Zoom calls, and face to face meetings, and I shared the screens for review as the app took shape.

  • Clear, flat navigation for a wide public audience
  • Plain labels and simple screen layouts
  • Consistent visual style across web and mobile
  • Service information grouped the way residents think

Selected screens

Screens from the resident mobile app and the admin portal, from sign up and home to service booking and user management. Click any screen to open it full size.

Challenges & solutions

Challenge: The app had to serve everyday residents of many ages and different comfort levels with phones. The client asked for minimum navigation, so people needed to reach education, health, and transport information without any confusion.

Solution: I kept the structure flat and the labels plain, grouped the services the way residents actually think about them, and reduced the taps needed to reach each service. This kept the app simple and easy for a wide public audience.

Conclusion

The app is live for city residents across a web portal and mobile apps for both iOS and Android. The simple, low-navigation interface makes it easy for a wide public audience to reach education, health, and transport information.

The design was delivered in about 3 months and the client shared a completion certificate. The clean, consistent screens gave residents an easy way to use the services every day.
